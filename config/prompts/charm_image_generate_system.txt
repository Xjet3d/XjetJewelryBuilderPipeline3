Every charm has exactly one attachment loop, and it is always the same simple loop.

The loop sits directly on the topmost point of the charm body, centered left to right, joined to the body by a short, solid, smooth connection.
The outer diameter of the loop is approximately one fifth of the height of the charm body.
The loop has the same metal, the same finish and a sturdy, even thickness all around.
The loop is plain: no texture, no engraving, no pattern, no twist, no decoration, no motif.
